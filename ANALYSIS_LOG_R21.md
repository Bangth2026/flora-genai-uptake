# Analysis log entry R21

Written in response to the same comments on the fifth version of the manuscript as R20, for the seventh version (22 November 2026), after test runs on small sets and before the full runs; committed with the results. All analyses are exploratory. No measure, class definition, parameter value or code of the attribution changes, and every earlier result is unchanged.

Two open instruction-tuned models are used as research instruments, never as part of the attribution: Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, the Q4_K_M GGUF files published by the Qwen team on Hugging Face, run with llama-cpp-python 0.3.37 on two CPU cores with greedy decoding (temperature 0, seed 0). Every text they generated is released, so the analyses can be rerun without the models.

## B. Benchmark (src/r21_benchmark.py)

B-46. Chatbot text revised by an open language model. Three routes are added at weight 0.05 each to the 13 specified routes (seed 2900, 100 simulated students). Each takes whole sentences of a borrowed reply (split as in R20 B-42, at most 150 tokens) and has Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct revise them as a student writing a data-science proposal would, under one of three instructions (light edit; rewrite in your own words; condense to about half); the revision is pasted in one insertion. Planted truth: a token of the revision is C if it lies in a run of at least four tokens aligned with the reply passage (difflib, no autojunk), T (writer's edit) otherwise. The move route draws its insertion point with int(random() * (start + 1)) instead of randrange(0, start + 1), so that the length of a revision does not change later draws; the set is built once to collect the passages, the revisions are generated and cached, and the set is rebuilt with them. Reported: C precision, recall, F1, student-level signed error of U and agreement on "at least half"; per instruction, the share of the revision in aligned runs of at least four and eight tokens, the share of revisions with no run of eight, the share of the true chatbot text labelled C and the share of the model's own words in each class.

## F. FLoRA (src/r21_flora_openmodel.py, src/r21_flora_example.py)

F-48. Labelled proposals for the article's Fig. 5: the proposals whose U is nearest the 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles of U (ties: length nearest the median), drawn token by token from the released labels; no text is shown.

F-49. An open model's replies to the writers' own prompts. For 50 students drawn at random (seed 2211), up to four prompts each (same seed) are sent to Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct with a neutral system prompt and no dialogue history (at most 256 new tokens). Per student: the share of the proposal's tokens of each released class covered by an 8-gram of these replies that occurs in none of the student's prompts; the same with the open model's replies to the next sampled student's prompts (chance control); the share covered by the GPT-4o replies logged for the same prompts; and the share of the open model's reply 8-grams that also occur in those GPT-4o replies. The open model is not the embedded GPT-4o, so this measures how much of each class is wording a different chatbot also produces verbatim for the same request, not the A3 rate.

Reproduce: `NSTUD=100 python -I src/r21_benchmark.py DATA R8_TOKEN_CLASSES WORK qwen2.5-3b-instruct-q4_k_m.gguf && python -I src/r21_flora_openmodel.py DATA WORK qwen2.5-1.5b-instruct-q4_k_m.gguf && python -I src/r21_flora_example.py results/fig5_flora_proposals.png` (R8_TOKEN_CLASSES as in R15-R20; WORK/r21_llm_cache.json and WORK/r21_openmodel_replies.json may be filled from the released .json.gz files to skip generation).

## Results

- B-46: C precision 0.996, recall 0.825, F1 0.902; U error -6.7 points (true mean C 39.3%); agreement at half 0.85. Revisions used: light 42, rewrite 37, condense 40 (of 240 generated while building). Share of the revision in aligned runs of at least 8 tokens: light 0.76, rewrite 0.34, condense 0.43; no such run: 0.10, 0.19, 0.25. True chatbot text labelled C: light 0.93, rewrite 0.64, condense 0.67 (verbatim paste 0.98, 10% replaced 0.67, 25% replaced 0.24 in the same set). The model's own words: X 0.95-0.97, C 0.006-0.054.
- F-48: 25th percentile: U 0.0%, X 89.7%; 50th: U 4.8%, T 73.0%, X 22.2%; 75th: U 42.7%, X 55.1% (all X-with-C); 90th: U 86.0%, X 4.7%.
- F-49: 191 prompts, mean open-model reply 184 tokens. Pooled share of each class covered by open-model 8-grams outside the student's prompts (own prompts | another student's prompts): C 0.24% | 0.00%; E 0.27% | 0.17%; S 11.1% | 0.00% (63 tokens); T 0.04% | 0.00%; X 0.08% | 0.00%. Mean share of a proposal covered: 0.16% (0.14% outside shared 8-grams) against 0.02% for the control; GPT-4o replies to the same prompts 17.2%; 0.38% of open-model reply 8-grams occur in those GPT-4o replies. Five of 50 proposals had any coverage (two in X); largest 3.1%.
