## Completing the withdrawal of the NIRVANA results

R26 withdrew the NIRVANA results and removed the NIRVANA scripts and outputs from the repository (release v1.26.0). Earlier tagged releases still contained them: v1.22.0 (four files) and v1.23.0, v1.24.0 and v1.25.0 (six files each). To complete the withdrawal:

- the GitHub releases and tags v1.22.0, v1.23.0, v1.24.0 and v1.25.0 were deleted. Everything else they contained is in release v1.27.0, whose results and logs reproduce every number reported for FLoRA and CoAuthor;
- Zenodo does not let a depositor change the files of a published version, so Zenodo was asked to restrict the files of the four corresponding archived versions;
- the copy of the NIRVANA data downloaded on 22 November 2026 has been deleted from the working environment; no other copy was kept.

The NIRVANA files remain only in the commit history of the branch, which was not rewritten, so that the commits cited by entries L1 to L26 keep their identifiers.
